Angko is a virtual pet game for iPhone. This policy explains how the current app, this website and support email handle your information.

Information collected during gameplay

Angko does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking or an app-operated online service. We do not collect or receive personal information through gameplay.

Game progress on your device

The app stores your pet's progress, unlocked items, room choices and audio settings locally on your device. These files let the game resume between sessions and calculate changes in your pet's needs while you are away.

Your operating system may include this data in device backups, according to your device and Apple account settings. Angko itself does not provide cloud synchronization.

Device permissions

Angko does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Removing your game data

Deleting Angko from your iPhone removes its local app data. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data. Copies in device backups are managed separately through your operating system and backup settings.

This website

This privacy and support page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by this page or embedded third-party content.

GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages websites. This hosting activity is separate from Angko's gameplay. See GitHub Pages' data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Opening this page from Angko launches your browser. Links to other websites are subject to those websites' own privacy practices.

Contacting support

If you email support, Lim Zhe Xun receives your email address and the information you choose to send in order to respond to your request. Support correspondence is processed by the email provider, Gmail, separately from the data stored locally by the game. See Google's Privacy Policy.

For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact limzhexun2000@gmail.com.

Policy updates

If future releases add data collection or online services, we will update this policy and provide any disclosures or choices required for those features. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

Need a hand?

For help with Angko, bug reports or feedback, get in touch by email.

limzhexun2000@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The Angko app version.
  • What happened and the steps that led to it.

There is no account to create or sign in to before contacting support.
